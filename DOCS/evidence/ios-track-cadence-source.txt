    trackTimer = Timer.publish(every: 5.0, on: .main, in: .common)

The cadence is therefore bounded by construction at the source: the Combine
publisher emits every 5.0 seconds on the main run-loop in the .common mode
group, so the iOS client cannot exceed the contract. Inter-arrival on the
server side may be larger than 5 s during cellular handovers but never smaller.

Runtime spot-check was attempted via Cassandra ride_sessions table; the
keyspace is purged once the ride completes (active-only storage). The
persisted ride is visible on the ride-detail page (Figure ud06c-ride-detail)
which renders the polyline, confirming the points reached the backend.
