## Functional Requirements

### Car Owner Experience
- **Mileage Tracking**: Clear visual representation of the current kilometers required to achieve a payout.
- **QR Scanning**: Seamless integration to confirm rides and track activity for companies and users.
- **Earnings Management**: Transparent display of earnings, with easy access to detailed payment history.
- **Find ad**: Easy way for drivers to find and join company marketing campaigns.

### Company Experience
- **Ad Placement Management**: Easy way to create and manage campaigns for selecting and renting available car ad spaces.
- **Campaign Statistics**: Real-time insights into ad performance, geographic reach, and financial metrics.
- **Driver Managment & Approval***: Tools to review, approve, or reject drivers who apply to a specific campaign based on criteria
